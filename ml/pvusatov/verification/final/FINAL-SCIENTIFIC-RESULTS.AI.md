# Frozen v3 FINAL scientific results

The single preselected sigma-0.8 ExtraTrees candidate **passes the preregistered primary recovery gate** against deployed sigma-1.2 v2. This is a recovery result on injected sources, not authorization to promote: the frozen summary leaves runtime and integration gates for separate coordinator review and records `promotion_authorized=false`. No alternate model, refit or reselection follows this FINAL result.

FINAL comprises **10 new native parent fields, eight physical exposures each**, with 270 correlated injected derivatives per parent (54 trajectories × five brightnesses), plus **2,000 independent synthetic fields** containing 3,998 injected truths. The synthetic set includes 1,600 truth-bearing fields and 400 fields with no positive truth. All 40 native and four synthetic caches completed without runtime/unknown-truth failures. Peak caps were not reached in any injected trial or the 40 native control views.

The primary minimum speed is 0.5 pixels/hour; slow secondary is 0.005. Each profile compares the same underlying latent sources and inputs between systems. Primary and slow attribution are paired separately; neither profile changes the other's denominator. All-planned includes unresolved motion and preexisting-control ambiguities. K1/K3 count distinct injected truths retained within one/three reviewed residual candidates per trial, not candidate classifications.

## Development evidence and limits

The packaged comet/Vesta cases and model-selection/validation fields are **DEVELOPMENT**, separate from the FINAL sets above. Comet D6's known target remains detected but falls from v2 model rank 3 to candidate rank 4, losing K3. Vesta D3's earlier published-list loss was corrected by the generic pre-final BLOOM policy: 99 residual entries plus the known unscored BLOOM at rank 100. The original negative report remains preserved; the same candidate weights were used. Native FINAL uses SWIR detectors 1–3 and does not establish MWIR D6 generalization.

Native real-control identities remain **UNKNOWN**: this experiment estimates injected-source recovery, not real precision, false-positive rates or confirmed-object probability. Ranking scores are uncalibrated; BLOOM evidence receives no model probability. Native sources were injected after registration with local native ePSFs, fixed source flux across passbands, and no added photon noise or variance update. Synthetic results depend on the frozen simulator and calibration bank.

The narrower detector alone is not a general ranking improvement: in primary native heuristic ordering K1 falls 93→41 and K3 falls 183→108, although the selected model improves the recovery counts above. More candidate rows and higher score magnitudes do not themselves indicate scientific accuracy. Runtime stage sums/worker RSS in the summary are separate evidence, not production service latency or a passed deployment gate.
