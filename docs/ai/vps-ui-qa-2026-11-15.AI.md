# VPS QA report: what users can and cannot trust

**Tested:** 15 November 2026, approximately 15:35–15:47 UTC / 18:35–18:47 Moscow time.

**Application:** <http://144.124.238.101:8080/>.

**Frontend and backend revision:** `758fb2905650dcd8050e6ae1ba226dbf481dcf01`.

**Scope:** live browser testing with Playwright, HTTP responses, read-only server inspection, service logs, and targeted source inspection at that revision. This PR contains the report and evidence, not application fixes.

## Read this first

The application can display a convincing scientific result even when the real search or measurement failed. A green state, a graph, a moving dot, a catalog match, or a successful export therefore does **not** currently establish that the requested observation was downloaded and analyzed.

This is the main release concern. It is more consequential than the visual scaling problems: a user can export invented measurements or inspect an image assigned to the wrong object without an adequate warning.

There are also working parts. The deployed Scala backend accepted real packaged `comet-d6` and `vesta-d6` sequences, completed inference, and returned valid image and difference PNGs. The default comet preset can display its prepared difference images. The problems below identify which other paths fail or misrepresent their result; they do not establish that the ML algorithm itself is globally broken.

**Triage:** 19 items — **3 P0**, **11 P1**, **5 P2**. Several share a root cause. These are 19 actionable triage items, not 19 unrelated implementation mistakes.

### What the priorities mean here

| Priority | Meaning for this product |
|---|---|
| **P0** | Blocks a scientific release: users can receive or export materially false evidence as a result for the requested target. This is a data-integrity classification, not a claim of a security exploit. |
| **P1** | A core workflow fails, operates on the wrong selection, or gives users an incorrect interpretation. Fix before asking a team to rely on that workflow. |
| **P2** | A usability, clarity, or control-design problem. Important, but it follows the data-integrity and workflow fixes. |

### Who owns what

**Frontend** owns browser state, source labels, rendering, controls, and exports. **Backend** owns the Scala API, durable jobs, deployment wiring, and public API contracts. **ML** owns inference semantics and the legacy FastAPI motion/catalogue service used by this UI. **Processing** owns cutouts, calibration, registration, and spectral measurement. These labels describe component responsibility, not individual blame.

In the table, the first component is the proposed lead. Additional components need to agree on the contract or help verify the fix.

## Findings with reproduction steps

<a id="qa-001"></a>
### QA-001 — P0: failed live acquisition becomes a successful-looking discovery

**Lead:** Frontend. **Also involved:** Backend and ML for the job/result contract.

**What a user sees:** a NASA/SPHEREx stream, “COORDINATE ML READY,” three tracks, a moving-object proof panel, a velocity of 28.4 arcsec/hour, and a five-epoch timeline. The real analysis has failed and has no observations.

**Reproduce:** open Sky Viewer, enter RA `250`, Dec `50`, Band 6, FOV `5'`, and press **Update Field**. Wait for the visible progress sequence to finish. The same failure pattern was observed earlier at RA `250`, Dec `10`.

**Observed evidence:** analysis `8a0faf83-47a1-48f4-a46f-f10ea0260f66` ended `failed / download_failed`. Its download `5a0303d7-1832-4ddd-a90d-cc24904a1e0d` ended `failed / archive_http_error`, with `observations: []` and `chunks: []`. Nevertheless, the browser displayed five `SPX-L3-RA250.00-DEC50.00-E…-A6` frames and three candidates. None of those frames had a residual image. The browser also attempted `/api/motion/detect` with the code's generated image tensor, rather than the downloaded observations.

The dates beginning `2025-06-15` are fallback constants. They are not a measured lower boundary of the archive.

**Expected / acceptance:** a failed or incomplete real job remains visibly failed or incomplete. Do not replace its observations, dates, track metrics, or discovery conclusion with generated content. Demonstration mode must be explicit and isolated from real-data results and export.

**Evidence:** [screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/11-custom-failed-but-ready.png), [final job states](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/custom-jobs.json), [browser trace](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/browser-evidence.json). Source: [App.tsx fallback and syntheticStack](../../frontend/src/App.tsx#L638), [fallback observations](../../frontend/src/services/spherexBackendApi.ts#L217).

### QA-002 — P0: failed spectral measurement still exports invented science

**Lead:** Frontend. **Also involved:** Processing for measurement availability and provenance.

**Reproduce:** open the 3I/ATLAS preset, select **Spectral Analysis**, wait for measurement to finish, then choose **Export CSV**.

**Observed:** `/api/spectrum?...candidate_id=3I%2FATLAS` returned HTTP 502 with `pipeline_upstream_error`. The UI still showed “102 Bands Calibrated,” an apparent spectrum, H₂O at SNR 14.8, and CO₂ at SNR 9.4. CSV export succeeded with 102 data rows, starting with flux 16.75 mJy at 0.75 µm. The fallback data originate from `getMock102BandSpectrum`; the molecule detections are hardcoded by target name. No real spectrum was obtained in this check.

This also needs correct empty-result handling: an actual measurement with no molecule detections must not be replaced by name-based molecular detections.

**Expected / acceptance:** after a failed measurement, show the error and unavailable measurements. Real-data CSV must require a successful result with observation provenance. Keep illustrative curves clearly separate, including in the downloaded file. A valid empty detection list remains empty.

**Evidence:** [spectrum screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/04-spectrum.png), [exported CSV](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/04-spectrum-export.csv), browser trace entry for `/api/spectrum`. Source: [SpectrumPlot.tsx](../../frontend/src/components/SpectrumPlot.tsx#L59), [mock spectrum generator](../../frontend/src/services/mockSpherexApi.ts#L1313).

### QA-003 — P0: Ceres and Barnard's Star share an identical observation image

**Lead:** Frontend.

**Reproduce:** from Home, open **(1) Ceres**, inspect its first frame, then open **Barnard's Star** and inspect its first frame.

**Observed:** Ceres is presented near RA `15.616171`, Dec `-5.471297`, with a July 2025 observation. Barnard's Star is presented near RA `269.452075`, Dec `4.693365`, with a June 2025 observation. Their first PNG files are byte-for-byte identical: **65,705 bytes**, SHA-256 `463a5f78201e318e1cf1af1139b2f1b1345f91d15e280816ccbd1cad5dfd4fb5`.

The Ceres branch directly assigns `REAL_BARNARD_FRAMES[idx].pngDataUrl`. The test proves incorrect reuse between the two targets; it does not independently certify the original astronomical provenance of the Barnard-labelled PNG.

**Expected / acceptance:** an observation image must belong to its stated field and observation ID. If a target has no verified sequence, show “data unavailable.” Add a provenance check that prevents another target's pixels from being relabelled as the requested object.

**Evidence:** [Ceres screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/16-ceres-image.png), [Barnard screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/17-barnard-image.png), [hash comparison](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/image-provenance-check.json), and both original browser PNG downloads in the evidence directory. Source: [Ceres frame assignment](../../frontend/src/services/mockSpherexApi.ts#L281).

<a id="qa-004"></a>
### QA-004 — P1: merely viewing a discussion initiates a submission

**Lead:** Frontend. **Also involved:** ML, which owns the motion/catalogue API.

**Reproduce:** open **Verification Feed**, then the first 3I/ATLAS entry with **Open in Sky Viewer & Verify**. No vote or submit click is required.

**Observed:** the client displayed “LEVEL 2 CONSENSUS ACHIEVED (12/10 Confirmations)” and attempted `POST /api/catalogue/auto-submit` with `confirmations: 12`. The component sets that starting count from the object's name and uses `Math.max(defaultBenchmarkVotes, explicitConfirmations)`.

**Important evidence limit:** the QA browser intercepted this POST before network delivery, and intercepted a second attempt when the component remounted. No clearinghouse submission was sent by this test. The confirmed defect is the automatic client attempt based on seeded confirmations; external delivery and acceptance were not tested.

**Expected / acceptance:** use independently persisted, server-validated confirmations. Viewing a record must not turn seeded discussion data into a publication request. Submission eligibility must be checked on the server, with an explicit authorized transition and duplicate prevention.

**Evidence:** [consensus screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/06-seeded-consensus.png), `blockedAutoSubmissions` in [browser evidence](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/browser-evidence.json). Source: [confirmation defaults and effect](../../frontend/src/components/CommunityVerificationStream.tsx#L115).

<a id="qa-005"></a>
### QA-005 — P1: the UI calls a service that is not running

**Lead:** Backend for deployment wiring. **Also involved:** ML and Frontend.

**Reproduce:** use live catalog pull, generic motion search, Verification Feed, or Candidate Catalog. Inspect their `/api/motion/*` and `/api/catalogue/*` requests.

**Observed:** these routes return HTTP 502. Nginx logs `connect() failed (111: Connection refused)` to `172.17.0.1:8001`. The server has three running containers: API, pipeline, and frontend. There is no port-8001 listener or running legacy ML service. All three existing containers are nevertheless marked healthy.

The deployed backend's separate `/api/analyses/motion` path works for packaged inputs. “ML is down” is therefore too broad: the UI's legacy motion/catalogue dependency is missing, while the installed inference path is operational.

**Expected / acceptance:** choose and deploy the supported service topology, then connect the browser to it. A deployment check should exercise the actual paths needed by the UI, not only `/health` and the HTML shell.

**Evidence:** [runtime snapshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/runtime-snapshot.txt), [API probes](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/initial-api-probes.json), [nginx excerpts](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/service-log-excerpts.log). Source: [nginx upstream](../../ops/frontend/nginx.conf#L30), [production compose](../../compose.yaml), [alternative service compose](../../compose.services.yaml).

<a id="qa-006"></a>
### QA-006 — P1 incident: fresh archive searches fail during this session

**Lead:** Backend for acquisition and error handling. **Also involved:** Processing for its independent archive client. **External dependency:** IRSA.

**Reproduce:** use the free-coordinate search described in QA-001, or request the spectrum for 3I/ATLAS.

**Observed:** native discovery returned `archive_http_error` or timeout. A direct request to the processing worker returned HTTP 502 and identified failure of `https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/SIA/capabilities`, itself returning `502 Proxy Error`. Backend logs contain the matching failed download ID. The public spectrum API reported `pipeline_upstream_error`.

**Interpretation:** this confirms an availability incident, not proof that the selected sky position has no observations. It also does not prove the repository caused IRSA's error. The UI defects that hide this condition are separate findings.

**Expected / acceptance:** preserve a retriable acquisition failure, retain job/error provenance, and allow retry when the upstream recovers. Do not turn an upstream failure into “no coverage” or a successful synthetic observation. Re-test a fresh real acquisition after the incident; cached/preset success alone does not close this item.

**Evidence:** [custom jobs](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/custom-jobs.json), [worker response](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/processing-spectrum-direct.txt), [backend and processing log excerpts](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/service-log-excerpts.log).

<a id="qa-007"></a>
### QA-007 — P1: Diff, Zodi, and BLOOM labels can describe products that do not exist

**Lead:** Frontend. **Also involved:** Processing and Backend for capability/result contracts.

**Reproduce:** select **Diff** on a generic failed search or a preset without residual images. Compare **Aligned** and **Zodi Clean**. Try BLOOM on an input without a BLOOM product.

**Observed:** six of the seven homepage presets in this session lacked residual products; the comet D6 preset had them. Generic fallback frames also lacked residuals. The canvas still permits Diff, enters a procedural renderer, and omits its generated static-star field. Zodi Clean can change CSS contrast while using the same input bitmap. BLOOM chooses `bloomDataUrl || residualDataUrl || imageDataUrl`, permitting ordinary pixels to appear under a mask label.

The legacy native-processing endpoints for epoch difference and zodiacal subtraction explicitly return HTTP 501 `unsupported_processing`. That honest API response is not itself a bug. The mismatch is the browser offering a successful-looking operation regardless of product availability. The separate inference endpoint can generate difference images; the two capabilities must not be conflated.

**Expected / acceptance:** a mode is enabled only for the corresponding returned artifact. Missing product means a clear unavailable state. Cosmetic contrast and fallback image selection must not be described as scientific processing.

**Evidence:** preset matrix and custom frame inventory in [browser evidence](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/browser-evidence.json), [capability probes](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/validation-and-capability-probes.json). Source: [canvas bitmap selection](../../frontend/src/components/CanvasViewport.tsx#L235).

<a id="qa-009"></a>
### QA-009 — P1: zoom breaks marker position and scale

**Lead:** Frontend.

**Reproduce:** open a field with multiple candidates and catalogue overlays; zoom out and then in. Watch the orange AllWISE labels, red candidate boxes, and labels near the HUD.

**Observed:** an unmatched `ctx.restore()` ends the outer zoom/pan transform after the first candidate. Subsequent candidates and catalogue overlays use identity coordinates while their fonts and radii still divide by zoom. This explains labels shrinking as the image grows and markers appearing in the wrong screen location.

A temporary browser-only trace confirmed it: first selected label had scale `1.232` and translation `(412, 444)`; `Detector Spike #3` and the AllWISE labels had scale `1` and translation `(0, 0)` in that same repaint. The trace wrapper was immediately restored.

**Expected / acceptance:** one consistent image-to-screen transform positions every source. UI labels/strokes remain legible in screen pixels. Verify zoom, pan, candidate selection, and overlay toggles together, including after a repaint.

**Evidence:** [zoomed out](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/12-overlay-zoom-out.png), [zoomed in](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/13-overlay-zoom-in.png), `overlayTransforms` in browser evidence. Source: [unmatched restore](../../frontend/src/components/CanvasViewport.tsx#L604).

### QA-010 — P1: the scientific HUD is largely hardcoded

**Lead:** Frontend. **Also involved:** Processing for verified metadata.

**Reproduce:** inspect Scientist mode in two different fields and at different zoom levels.

**Observed:** surface flux `12.43 MJy/sr`, SNR `18.4`, PSF `1.42 px`, zodiacal model `DIRBE v4.1`, WCS matrix `CD1_1: -1.72e-3`, and calibration `±1.8%` are component literals. Initial coordinate text and grid labels also have fixed values. Pointer coordinates and the scale bar use a fixed nominal 6.2 arcsec/pixel rather than the selected image WCS. For example, the custom field at RA 250°, Dec 50° can initially show coordinate text near Barnard's Star.

**Expected / acceptance:** show measured metadata with units and provenance, or show unavailable. Cursor coordinates must come from the actual displayed grid and its WCS. Distinguish detector sampling from the current screen zoom. Do not attach precision or calibration claims to constants.

**Evidence:** [custom-field screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/11-custom-failed-but-ready.png), [Scientist screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/01-barnard-scientist.png). Source: [HUD literals](../../frontend/src/components/CanvasViewport.tsx#L998), [coordinate conversion](../../frontend/src/components/CanvasViewport.tsx#L140).

<a id="qa-018"></a>
### QA-018 — P2: model and score presentation do not identify the running analysis

**Lead:** Frontend. **Also involved:** ML.

**Observed:** the viewer presents “Random Forest,” training on 400 synthetic fields, percentage-style Active/Inspect values, and timed “ML analysis” progress for presets. The deployed backend reports the installed model as `spherex-v4-development-fc3378f486961a8c`, family `RankNetTreeBoost`, and score kind `sigmoid_pairwise_utility`, explicitly not a calibrated probability. Preset display uses stored review results rather than submitting a new v4 inference request.

The existence of another legitimate heuristic/RF implementation is not the defect. The UI needs to identify which implementation and result it is actually showing.

**Expected / acceptance:** display model ID/version, algorithm, result source, and score meaning from the returned analysis metadata. Label stored benchmarks as stored benchmarks. Do not imply a newly executed inference, discovery probability, or updated ranking merely because an animation finished.

**Evidence:** [installed model and completed jobs](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/analysis-jobs.json), [preset screenshot](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/02-real-comet-preset.png), browser request trace. Source: [preset progress and finalization](../../frontend/src/App.tsx#L485).

<a id="qa-019"></a>
### QA-019 — P2: invalid declination is silently replaced with another coordinate

**Lead:** Frontend.

**Reproduce:** enter RA `361`, Dec `91`, and press **Update Field**.

**Observed:** the UI starts a search at RA `1`, Dec `90`, without explaining the correction. RA wrapping is a reasonable convention; silently clipping an invalid declination changes the sky position requested by the user.

**Expected / acceptance:** reject declination outside −90°…+90° with a field-level message, or clearly explain any correction before executing a different search. Keep RA normalization explicit. Backend invalid-coordinate validation already correctly returns HTTP 422.

**Evidence:** `invalidInput` in [browser evidence](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/browser-evidence.json), [backend validation probe](evidence/vps-qa-20261115/validation-and-capability-probes.json). Source: [coordinate form](../../frontend/src/components/LeftControlPanel.tsx#L67).

## Server findings in plain language

There were no signs of CPU, RAM, or disk exhaustion in the captured snapshot. The API used approximately 300 MiB, the processing worker approximately 78 MiB, and frontend nginx only a few MiB. Root filesystem usage was about 41%. This was not a load test.

The missing port-8001 service explains repeated browser 502 responses. The failing IRSA capabilities/search requests explain fresh-acquisition and spectrum failures. Healthy container badges do not cover either condition. Backend logs correctly record failed download IDs, while browser fallback code hides their meaning from the user.

No logs from a running legacy ML container could be inspected because that container was absent. This is an explicit evidence limit, not evidence that its model threw an exception.

Processing logs also contain repeated Astropy warnings about SIP distortion coefficients without the matching `-SIP` CTYPE suffix, plus unsupported HTTP-upgrade warnings. These deserve a **Processing** follow-up, but this session did not measure a resulting coordinate error or demonstrate a broken WebSocket feature. They are retained as investigation leads, not counted as additional confirmed bugs. Validate image registration against the original frame WCS before changing distortion handling; a warning alone does not justify removing coefficients.

## Suggested repair order

1. Stop presenting or exporting generated scientific results as live measurements. Separate demonstrations from real data. Remove cross-target image reuse.
2. Disable automatic submission based on seeded votes; make consensus and validation authoritative on the server.
3. Align the deployed service topology and browser API paths. Restore real acquisition when the upstream is available and make failures visible meanwhile.
4. Gate every processing mode and detector choice on actual returned products and metadata.
5. Fix canvas transforms, coordinate metadata, dossier identity, and mobile playback hit areas.
6. Simplify the spectral controls, HUD, timeline, and model/score labels.

These are proposed fixes and acceptance targets. This documentation PR does not implement or deploy them.

## Coverage, artifacts, and limits

The browser session covered all seven homepage targets; all six detector buttons and All Bands on the comet preset; channel-slider extremes; raw/clean/difference modes; desktop playback; catalogue pull; spectral measurement and export; feed-to-viewer navigation; catalogue/dossier/export navigation; direct-link reload; custom coordinates; zoom extremes; and mobile panes/hit areas at `390×844`. No votes, comments, external submissions, deployments, service restarts, or application fixes were performed. The unexpected auto-submit requests were intercepted by the QA browser before network delivery. Not covered: load/concurrency limits, restart persistence, cache eviction under disk pressure, full security testing, every catalogue filter combination, actual external clearinghouse delivery, or a scientific precision/recall benchmark.
